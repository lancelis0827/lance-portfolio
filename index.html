About Me

I graduated with a degree in Information Technology, and during that journey I realized that completing projects wasn't enough if I didn't truly understand how they worked. That realization led me to slow down, rebuild my foundations, and focus on learning through real-world projects.

As I continue rebuilding my foundations, I aim to write software that is readable, maintainable, well documented, and developed with security in mind so that it remains understandable for both users and developers.
